Welcome

Feel at home in historic Pisa

You are staying in a historic apartment overlooking the Arno, in the heart of Pisa. Take your time, walk the Lungarni and let the city reveal itself on foot.

Check-in

Standard check-in from 18:00. If you arrive earlier, please ask us about luggage drop-off and the earliest possible access.

Historic home

This is a historic 14th-century apartment. Some of the people represented in the paintings displayed in the apartment stayed here.

By car

Parking made simple

For unloading luggage, we recommend bringing your bags to the apartment first and then moving the car to a suitable parking area. Always check the road signs on the day of arrival.

Lungarno Guadalongo

Closest practical option • outdoor parking

€0.75/hour • paid generally 08:00–17:00 in the regulated area. The official tourism portal also lists a free parking area at Lungarno Guadalongo; availability and exact spaces can vary.

Open in Maps

Saba Vittorio Emanuele II

Covered / structured parking • central

Open 24/7. Current published rate: about €2.90/hour; parking tariff listed at €3.00/hour and €19/day. Online H24 multi-day rate from €19, plus possible booking fee.

Parking details

Tower Parking

Outside the ZTL • useful for the Tower area

Mon–Sat 07:00–20:30; Sun 09:00–20:30. Maximum vehicle height published: 2.00 m. Check the operator's current tariff before parking.

Open in Maps

Piazza dei Miracoli

Useful when visiting the Tower

Parking at Via Carlo Salomone Cammeo 51. 06:30–23:30 every day, including holidays. About 350 car spaces; not for campers, vans or tourist buses.

Official info
ZTL reminder: Pisa has regulated limited-traffic areas. Do not follow a route blindly into the historic centre. Check the signs and current restrictions before entering; parking authorization does not automatically mean access to every ZTL. Official information is available from PisaMo and the City of Pisa.
